# Academy artwork

Rook is the established Letter Quest character: green jacket, gold tie, glasses, dark hair and moustache. `rook.mjs` preserves that identity with independently animated eyes, head, mouth, moustache and hand. The rook icon is an original SVG drawing in `icon.svg`. The current rounded chess pieces derive from RhosGFX’s CC0 set; [source, license and modifications](pieces/PROVENANCE.md).

`academy-world.png` was created with the built-in image-generation tool on September 14, 2026. No third-party game artwork or character references were supplied. It is retained as a historical asset and is no longer displayed. The current path and play surface use plain backgrounds; the board, pieces and character reactions take priority.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset: original background illustration for a polished children's chess learning game, Rook Academy. Create a tall portrait illustrated storybook landscape, 1024x1536, with premium clean mobile game art direction: sculptural rounded shapes, beautifully controlled cel shading, crisp silhouettes, subtle fine grain. An inviting chess academy island: a friendly cream stone rook-shaped castle with terracotta roofs at upper right, a small chess garden with big knight topiary at lower left, little warm lanterns, carved stone steps, soft green trees and rolling grass, a turquoise stream at bottom right crossed by a stone bridge. NO CHARACTERS. NO TEXT. Composition specifically for an interactive lesson path: keep the middle vertical 50% of the image mostly open very pale warm ivory ground with subtle curved path suggestions; all detailed scenic vignettes cluster at far left and right edges. The central empty ground must run continuously from top to bottom, softly fading to near white. Airy and joyful, warm sunlight, deep forest green and turquoise with honey gold, orange and cream accents. Flat orthographic storybook/isometric hybrid, no photographic realism, no purple gradients, no generic UI, no buttons, no stars or progress markers, no chessboard, no watermark. Distinct original environment; do not use Duolingo logos or characters. Image should be readable and beautiful as a tall learning path background with real HTML lesson buttons laid on top.

The game-choice previews in `../previews/` are captures of the actual games using isolated generic demonstration profiles. They contain no household saves or private profile names. Screenshots were resized and JPEG-encoded for fast local delivery.
