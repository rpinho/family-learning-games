# Calm study assets

The calm SVG illustrations are original vector drawings made for this project. The water bed reuses the project’s original synthetic water sound from the calm-table study, encoded as AAC at 64 kbps (162,722 bytes), with a −20 LUFS source target. It is optional, gesture-loaded, pauses in hidden tabs and lowers during speech. No reference-app graphics or audio are included.

This is a review-only visual idea. Existing exercise, assistance and save rules remain authoritative.
