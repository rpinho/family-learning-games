# Field-book study assets

Original SVG field illustrations and CSS, authored for this review-only study. The quiet water reuses this project's synthetic 20-second AAC loop (64 kbps, 162,722 bytes, source target −20 LUFS). No reference-app media. Water is optional, gesture-loaded, pauses in hidden pages, and ducks during recorded speech. Existing learning, motion and save rules are retained.
